# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Recruiters (often non-technical), hiring managers, and other engineers evaluating my QA engineering skills, mostly for remote US roles. The top of the page has to make sense to anyone in seconds; the technical depth sits below for those who want it. All of them are looking for clear evidence of technical competence, problem-solving, and attention to detail, and most want to end by downloading the CV or getting in touch.

## Product Purpose
To showcase my professional experience, skills, and projects as a Senior QA Engineer. It serves as an interactive resume that demonstrates both my technical capabilities and my aesthetic sensibilities.

## Brand Personality
High-craft, cinematic, dark-mode-first (with a light option), kinetic, calm. Professional yet technologically sophisticated, with personality carried by type and motion rather than ornament.

## Anti-references
Generic portfolio templates, light mode as the default, overly complex navigation, "AI slop" or cliche SaaS designs: decorative or invented metrics (real figures from the CV are fine), identical card grids used as the whole page structure, fake product chrome such as mock dashboards or window dots.

## Design Principles
- Practice what you preach (high quality, bug-free).
- Show, don't tell: a real artefact beats a description of one.
- Kinetic minimalism (motion adds value, not noise).
- Expert confidence.
- Accessibility is non-negotiable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA compliant, readable contrast in dark mode, clear focus states, and considerations for reduced motion.
